## Report a bug or ask for a feature

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/a6n-ai/rays/issues). Include your Rays version (Rays > About Rays), your macOS version and the steps that cause the problem.

## Email

Write to [hi@a6n.ai](mailto:hi@a6n.ai). We usually reply within a few days.
